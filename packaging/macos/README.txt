Spotless @VERSION@ for macOS
A minimal Spotify player in the spirit of Winamp. It needs Spotify Premium.
For Macs with Apple silicon or Intel, macOS @MINIMUM@ or newer.

Install
  Drag Spotless.app to Applications. Or don't install it: it runs from
  anywhere, a USB drive too.

  Spotless isn't signed by Apple, so macOS won't open it the first time
  ("Apple could not verify..."): choose Done, then open System Settings >
  Privacy & Security and, near the bottom, choose "Open Anyway" for
  Spotless. It opens normally after that.

Update
  Spotless doesn't update itself on macOS yet: a newer release's
  Spotless.app replaces this one.

Uninstall
  Move Spotless.app to the Bin. Your settings, playlists and sign-in are
  kept in ~/Library/Application Support/spotless, and songs played in
  ~/Library/Caches/spotless; delete those folders to remove them too.

Keys
  F1 lists the keys, F10 opens the settings (on a Mac keyboard, hold fn
  for them). ⌘Q quits.
